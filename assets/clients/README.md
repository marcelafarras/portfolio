# Brand logo assets

19 approved strip entries. Autograph / Luminary Tower was removed at Marcel’s request.

MasukKampus product marks retain their original colour. Other logos use supplied white SVG originals or SVG display wrappers over the original artwork. Wrappers use SVG alpha/luminance filters and crop transparent/blank outer padding; they preserve source shapes and lettering without AI redrawing. A wrapper embedding a PNG/JPEG is not a newly vectorised logo and retains the source resolution.

- Agora: the current official mark with “Lifestyle Mall”, from `https://agoramall.co.id/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/agora-lifestyle-mall-logo-white.png`.
- Ballroom: the profile image displayed at `https://www.instagram.com/thamrinnineballroom/`, downloaded 12 November 2026. The available original is 150 × 150px. Its blue field is removed through a luminance mask for the white display variant.
- Chubb Square: the CHUBB image explicitly supplied and approved by Marcel. Its white field is removed through an inverse-luminance mask.
- Smart Panda and String & Space: white SVG originals supplied by Marcel in the intake folders.
- Other originals and official source records remain in the local Brand Logos intake folder.

The separate `WEB READY` intake folder contains the same display assets and a manifest. The main site stays unchanged until publication is approved.
